The Property

An unhurried stay in the Wayanad wilderness.

Idam was founded as a quiet refuge for travelers who appreciate nature in its unembellished state. Situated near the river delta of Kuruvadweep, the homestay overlooks a tranquil pond framed by indigenous trees and spice foliage.

There are no crowded corridors or impersonal check-in counters here. Guests wake to the rustle of bamboo, share homemade puttu or appams around the timber dining table, and spend evenings by a gentle campfire under dark, clear skies.

The Destination

Around Kuruvadweep & Wayanad

A few of our favorite nearby trails, wildlife sanctuaries, and waters to explore during your stay.

5 mins drive

Kuruvadweep Island

Protected river delta along the Kabini. Walk through evergreen bamboo trails and experience traditional raft crossings.

20 mins drive

Tholpetty Wildlife

Morning and dusk open jeep safaris with frequent sightings of wild elephants, deer herds, and peacocks.

45 mins drive

Banasura Sagar Dam

Picturesque earthen dam with speed boating set against the misty backdrop of the Banasura hills.

Day trip

Chembra Peak

A highland trek passing through tea estates to reach the famous heart-shaped perennial mountain lake.
